# Business Decision Toolkit

**Explore online:** [Open the public browser experience](https://luckygithub777.github.io/business-decision-toolkit/). The public web companion illustrates decision methods; it does not automate business or financial decisions.

Three fill-in templates for improving the evidence behind practical business choices.

- [Business Model Review](templates/01_business_model_review_template.md) — define the offer, customer value, costs and operating risks.
- [Forecast Assumptions Register](templates/03_forecast_assumptions_register_template.md) — separate base, downside and upside cases from verified demand.
- [Decision Quality Checklist](templates/04_decision_quality_checklist_template.md) — examine alternatives, assumptions, stewardship and stop conditions.

Open a template and replace blanks using information you have permission to use. An unknown result is a prompt for investigation, not proof of readiness. This kit does not provide financial, tax, legal or investment advice and does not automate decisions. Never share client or personal records in example completions.
